# SIH 2026: Official 6-Slide Pitch Deck Image Generation Master Guide
## Full-Slide Widescreen (16:9) Prompts — Clean Vector Graphics — Zero Gibberish Text

This guide gives you **crystal-clear, full-slide-sized image prompts** for all 6 official SIH slides, along with exact instructions to ensure your AI tool generates a **true 16:9 widescreen slide** that covers your whole slide instead of a tiny square.

---

## 🚀 How to Get Full-Slide (16:9) Size in Every AI Tool

Depending on which AI tool you are using, follow these simple steps so it never generates a square:

| Tool | How to Get Full-Slide 16:9 Widescreen |
|---|---|
| **ChatGPT / DALL-E 3** | The prompt starts with `Create a full-screen 16:9 widescreen landscape presentation slide image`. ChatGPT will automatically set the aspect ratio to widescreen `1792x1024`. |
| **Midjourney** | Just copy the prompt with the `--ar 16:9` tag at the end. |
| **Canva / Bing / Leonardo** | Select **16:9 (Landscape)** in the dimension settings before pasting the prompt. |

---

## 🎨 Design Rules Built into Every Prompt

Every prompt below automatically includes:
- **Slide Coverage:** Full-slide 16:9 landscape filling the entire width from left to right.
- **Background:** 100% Solid Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with zero gray borders.
- **Palette:** Navy Blue (`#1B3B6F`), Forest Green (`#3E9B4F`), Alert Red (`#D6473C`), Amber Gold (`#E8A33D`).
- **Typography:** **Zero text in image**. It leaves clean, blank white cards for you to type your actual text in PowerPoint.
- **Style:** Clean 2D flat vector corporate infographic (no 3D, no realistic photos, no blurry sketches).

## 💡 How to Add Your Text in PowerPoint

1. **Paste the prompt** into your AI image tool (ChatGPT / Midjourney / Canva / Bing) and download the generated 16:9 widescreen image.
2. **Insert the image** onto your PowerPoint slide (it will fit the slide 16:9 layout).
3. **Insert Text Boxes** directly over the clean white rectangular boxes in PowerPoint to type your bullet points, numbers, and titles.
